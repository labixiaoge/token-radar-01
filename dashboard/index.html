How the value is estimated

Implied value = TVL × category ratio × growth factor × age factor. The category ratio is the median market-cap/TVL of the tokenized protocols in that category — Dexs ~0.35, Lending ~0.13, Derivatives ~1.60 — and a category with no median falls back to the global median 0.308. The ratio used is clamped to [0.05, 2.0]: Prediction Market’s raw 7.36 is clamped to 2.0. Growth follows the 7-day TVL change, up to 1.5× and down to 0.75×. Age: under 6 months 0.9×, over 2 years 1.1×. Snapshot sealed 2026-11-14, 758 protocols at $5M+. A rough yardstick, not a forecast — it says a protocol records no token, never that it has none.
