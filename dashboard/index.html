How the value is estimated

Implied value = TVL × category ratio × growth factor × age factor. The category ratio is the median market-cap/TVL of the tokenized protocols in that category, read from this very snapshot — Dexs ~0.34, Lending ~0.13, RWA ~0.27, Cross Chain Bridge ~1.22 — and a category with fewer than three tokenized protocols falls back to the global median 0.169. The ratio used is clamped to [0.05, 2.0]. Growth follows the 7-day TVL change, up to 1.5× and down to 0.75×. Age: under 6 months 0.9×, over 2 years 1.1×. The board ranks only genuine launch candidates: it leaves out centralised exchanges, bridges, wrapped and staked products and versioned duplicates, the same rule the snapshot’s watchlist uses.
