How the implied value is estimated. For each category we measured the median market-cap / TVL ratio across the protocols in that category that already have a token (for example Dexs ≈ 0.35, Lending ≈ 0.13, Derivatives ≈ 1.60). A protocol's implied launch value is its TVL times that ratio, clamped to [0.05, 2.0]; categories with no measured ratio use the global median of 0.308. Prediction Market's raw ratio (7.36) is clamped to 2.0. The snapshot is sealed: as of 2026-11-14 it holds 758 protocols at or above $5M TVL. This is a rough, comparable yardstick — not a price forecast. A launch is never guaranteed; farm at your own risk and never deposit more than you can lose.
